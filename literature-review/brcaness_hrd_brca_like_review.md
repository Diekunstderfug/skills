# BRCAness/HRD/BRCAlike Literature Review 2023-2026

## Key Findings

### BRCAness in Breast Cancer

Miller et al. (2026) conducted a phase 1 study of ASTX727 plus talazoparib in TNBC patients, showing that DNMTi+PARPi therapy was effective in both TNBC and hormone resistant (HRBC) models with intact BRCA by inducing a "BRCAness phenotype"^1.

Murai and Pommier (2023) provided a comprehensive review on BRCAness, HRD, and synthetic lethality, highlighting how genomic profiling can identify BRCAness and discussing the utility of evaluating BRCAness/HRD to select therapies with PARP inhibitors and platinum derivatives^2.

Jafari et al. (2026) demonstrated that pharmacological targeting of receptor tyrosine kinases (RTKs) can induce an HRD-like or "BRCAness" state, causing synthetic lethality and sensitizing otherwise resistant tumors to PARP inhibitors^3.

Zhang et al. (2026) showed that repression of PRMT (protein arginine methyltransferase) activities significantly enhanced PARP inhibitor-induced DNA damage in HR-proficient ovarian and breast cancer cells by downregulating DNA damage repair genes and BRCAness-associated pathways^4.

### Homologous Recombination Deficiency (HRD) in Breast Cancer

Kozan et al. (2026) performed whole genome sequencing on 50 tumor samples from patients with locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer, finding that HRD (confident or suspected) was detected in 27% of patients, enriched in TNBC (60%) versus Luminal tumors (17%)^5.

Yndestad et al. (2023) studied HRD prevalence across breast cancer subtypes, showing that strict HRD criteria was present in 5% of non-TNBC cases, contrasting 47% of the TNBC tumors^6.

Llop-Guevara et al. (2026) evaluated RAD51-based HRD assessment in 291 early-stage breast cancer patients, finding HRD by RAD51 in 78.4% of tumors, with higher pCR rates (52.3% vs 36.4%) and better 5-year OS (89.2% vs 82.8%) in HRD tumors^7.

Davies et al. (2026) analyzed 502 ER-positive/HER2-negative tumors by whole genome sequencing, showing that HRD was much less frequent in ER-positive/HER2-negative BC (8.4%) compared to TNBC^8.

### BRCA-like Phenotypes

Sottnik et al. (2026) revealed that invasive lobular carcinoma (ILC) cells exhibit a "BRCA-like" state lacking key HR proteins, consistent with HR dysfunction but distinct from classic "BRCAness," and PARP inhibitor talazoparib produced durable growth suppression^9.

Schouten et al. (2024) demonstrated in the PAOLA-1 trial that patients with a BRCA-like tumor had significantly longer progression-free survival after olaparib treatment (36.4 vs 18.6 months; HR, 0.49)^10.

Rodler et al. (2023) conducted the S1416 trial showing that the addition of veliparib to cisplatin significantly improved progression-free survival in patients with BRCA-like metastatic TNBC (5.9 vs 4.2 months; HR 0.57; p=0.010)^11.
